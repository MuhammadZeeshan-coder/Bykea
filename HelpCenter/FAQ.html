We've Got The Answers

We’ve answered some of our most common queries but you can contact us directly for any further information. Our call center representatives are available for you 24/7. Call us at 021 38654444 or you can email us at cs@bykea.com

Customers:

I got into an accident, now what?
Whether you are a rider or a driver, we have you covered. Your safety is our utmost priority, contact us at +92 21-38654444 to find out your coverage.

Can I use BYKEA without a smartphone?
Yes you can! We pride ourselves for making services available for everyone in Pakistan. Just give us a call at +92 307-1234567 to book your Bykea partner.

How can I start a BYKEA Franchise?
Owning a Bykea franchise offers a unique opportunity of being your own boss and empowering your local community. To read details about how to become a Bykea Franchisee, visit our Bykea Franchise page.

How do I unlock by Bykea Account?
Unlocking your Bykea Account is easy. Download the Jazz Cash App or go to the nearest Jazz Cash Kiosk to unlock your account.

How do I create an account?
Download the Bykea App for your Google Play Store or Apple App Store, verify your phone number, and you are ready to book your first ride.

Partners:

How do I become a Bykea Partner?
Applying to be a Bykea partner is simple. Download the Bykea Partner app or visit the nearest Bykea Franchise to register. For more details visit the Bykea Partner Page.

I want to become a Bykea Partner but don’t own a bike.
We will connect you with a financial institution that provides easy installment plans or leases. In some areas, a Bykea partner can earn up to 5 times the monthly installment – making owning a bike easy.

Are Bykea Partners insured?
Safety of our customers and partners is the utmost priority. We provide our partners and customers an option of getting themselves insured during the ride.

How will the loan be disbursed?
We will disburse the loan into your Bykea Wallet that you can withdraw into any bank or branchless banking account to advance the cash.

Why are other Driver-Partners offered a higher or lower Upfront Cash amount?
The Upfront Cash amount offered to each Driver-Partner under the program is determined based on multiple factors including the partner’s past incentive earnings and driving or delivery history.

Business:

My parcel got damaged during delivery, now what?
Our Bykea partners are trained and understand the importance of delivering your items with care. However, we provide an added layer of safety through an opt-in insurance for parcels so even if there is any odd chance that it gets damaged, your valuable items are completely protected. To find out details about how to claim your coverage, contact us at +92 21 38654444.

How can I start a Bykea Franchise?
Owning a Bykea franchise offers a unique opportunity of being your own boss and empowering your local community. To read details about how to become a Bykea Franchisee, visit our Bykea Franchise page.

Does Bykea have business solutions?
If you are looking to ship multiple items at once instantly, our Business Panel will be perfect for you. Read more about the advantages and how to get it on our Delivery for Business page.
